# STAGE 6 — Payments-as-Credit-Identity

**Priority:** Core

## Framing

**Verified Transaction Record** — a verified history of completed, on-time orders that a bank or NBFC could choose to review.  
Not a credit score, not a credit report, not a financial product or guarantee.

Forbidden in UI/copy: “credit score”, “CIBIL”, “loan”.

## Metrics (Stage 4 Settlement Released only)

- Total verified completed orders
- Verified income (trailing 12 months — say so if less history)
- On-time settlement rate
- Tenure on the platform

## Deliverable

- Money tab card + shareable `/record/[shareId]`
- Print / Save as PDF via browser
